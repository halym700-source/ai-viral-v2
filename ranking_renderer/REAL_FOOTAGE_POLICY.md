# ReshRanks: REAL-FOOTAGE-ONLY POLICY

The channel owner has explicitly ruled out AI-generated footage.

## Mandatory rules

1. Every published shot must originate from a real camera recording. No generative video, synthetic imagery, AI animation, image-to-video, or AI-created visual insert.
2. Discovery may research public YouTube Shorts, TikTok and Instagram Reels, but discovery does not grant download, remix, repost, or monetization rights.
3. Use only original owner footage, footage with suitable licensing, or footage with documented creator permission. Verify embedded audio/music separately.
4. The editing pipeline may crop, cut, color-adjust, resize and add text, numbers and voiceover to real clips. Those editing operations are not AI-generated footage.
5. The metadata filter in source_pexels.py rejects explicitly labelled AI-generated footage, but this is NOT a guarantee that a clip is genuine. Manual verification is required for uncertain material.
6. If five eligible, authentic clips cannot be verified, stop the job; do not silently substitute AI-generated video or unlicensed viral clips.
7. Keep all uploads PRIVATE for owner review. Do not enable automatic public posting without explicit owner instruction.
8. Pexels is one possible source, not an internet-wide video index. Do not claim that the existing pipeline searches all public social videos. A broader rights-aware discovery module must be built and validated separately.
